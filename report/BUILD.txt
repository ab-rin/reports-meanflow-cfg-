report_for_submission - LaTeX source

Compile from this folder:
  tectonic report_for_submission.tex

Alternatively:
  pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error report_for_submission.tex
  pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error report_for_submission.tex

Keep report_for_submission_assets beside the .tex file.
The checked PDF is two A4 pages, including references.

The report describes the final team_06 four-NFE implementation and the
EMA checkpoint at update 101800. Experimental results and original sample
grids come from the supplied 101800 evidence bundle. The training-loss plot
uses the same logged values with a shortened axis label to prevent clipping.
The sample grids are unmodified; LaTeX displays their first two rows.
